# Ridership by platform

Average weekday riders with fare evaders, over 103 weekdays from 2025-08-19 to 2025-12-22 (`data/raptor/manifest.csv`).
The 2019 column scales the OD data on these dates to 2019's counted station entries.
The peak hour is the hour, by when it starts, with the most riders in the Aug-Dec 2025 column on its trains there (getting on or off at a place, boarding a route, or joining a line), and those riders.
Platforms are from OpenStreetMap's subway routes (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), by `mta-od-data fetch-platforms`.
A platform's area is its OpenStreetMap outline's, and its On + Off per m² is its riders getting on or off over the day, or the peak hour, per m² of it: how busy it is for its size, not how many stand on it at once.

Produced by `mta-od-data ridership --by platform --markdown-out src/mta_od_data/ridership/by_platform.md`.
