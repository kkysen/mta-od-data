# Ridership by platform

Average weekday riders with fare evaders, over 22 weekdays from 2025-09-16 to 2025-09-22 (as `raptor assign-range` routes them).
The 2019 column scales the OD data on these dates to 2019's counted station entries.
The peak hour is the hour, by when it starts, with the most riders in the Sep 2025 column on its trains there (getting on or off at a place, boarding a route, or joining a line), and those riders.
Platforms are from OpenStreetMap's subway routes (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), by `mta-od-data fetch-platforms`.
A platform's area is its OpenStreetMap outline's, and its On + Off per m² is its riders getting on or off over the day, or the peak hour, per m² of it: how busy it is for its size, not how many stand on it at once.

Produced by `mta-od-data ridership --by platform --month 2025-09 --markdown-out src/mta_od_data/ridership/2025-09/by_platform.md`.
